Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how KELTO ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects the personal information of customers who visit our website or place an order with us.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide at checkout: name, email, phone number, and shipping address.
  • Order information: items purchased, order value, and order history.
  • Payment information processed securely by Razorpay — we do not store your card, UPI, or bank details on our servers.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and device information, used to keep the site working correctly.

How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To communicate order updates, shipping confirmations, and tracking details.
  • To respond to customer support requests.
  • To improve our products, website, and shopping experience.

Sharing your information

We share your information only with trusted third parties necessary to fulfil your order — such as our payment gateway (Razorpay) and courier/shipping partners. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. Payment transactions are handled entirely by Razorpay's secure, PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or request deletion of your data, subject to legal and accounting requirements, by contacting us below.
