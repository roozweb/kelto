Shipping Policy

Last updated:

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation. You'll receive an email once your order has shipped.

Shipping charges

We offer free shipping across India on all orders over ₹. Orders below this amount are charged a flat shipping fee at checkout.

Delivery timelines

Most orders are delivered within 2–10 business days after dispatch, depending on your location. Remote areas may take slightly longer.

Order tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link via email/SMS so you can follow it to your door.

Delays

Occasionally, factors outside our control — weather, courier delays, or regional restrictions — may affect delivery times. We'll keep you updated if this happens.

Undelivered or lost packages

If your tracking shows delivered but you haven't received your order, or if a package appears lost in transit, contact us within 7 days so we can investigate with our courier partner.
